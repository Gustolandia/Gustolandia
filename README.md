# Gustavo Pedro Ricou

**Backend Engineer at BoscaSports | Machine Learning & Statistics Researcher at TCD**

I build backend systems for live data and am completing the MSc in Statistics &
Data Science at Trinity College Dublin. My research interests connect machine
learning, statistical reasoning and scientific computing, informed by earlier
work in physics and instrumentation.

## Current Research

My MSc thesis studies **gradient interference and transfer in multi-task
learning**: when does a shared neural-network trunk help or hurt each task,
compared with matched single-task baselines? The work is in progress; the
[thesis overview](https://gustavopedroricou.com/research) explains the setting and
research questions. I am seeking ML/AI or statistics PhD opportunities from
**July 2027**.
